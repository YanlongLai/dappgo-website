TL;DR: TW Stocks, US Stocks, Options Explorer, DappGo News, and Number Merge have different data flows. In the three research apps, “Optional detailed usage analytics” is on by default when you have not made a choice, and you can turn it off in Settings anytime; Firebase Crashlytics crash reports follow the same switch. When you turn on push notifications, the app stores a push token (Apple APNs / Google FCM) and an anonymous Firebase uid on our backend. Ad removal and subscriptions are paid through Apple or Google and verified by RevenueCat; the US Stocks valuation page sends the anonymous RevenueCat App User ID to the DappGo data service to confirm a subscription. Advertising choices are handled separately by Google UMP, Apple ATT, and device settings. AI research uses public market data only.

Research apps: analytics, crash reports and your choices

This section applies to the iOS and Android versions of Options Explorer, US Stocks, and TW Stocks that show “Optional detailed usage analytics” in Settings. Google Firebase Analytics uses Advanced Consent Mode: the SDK starts with all four consent purposes denied by default and is then updated from your choice. Existing explicit choices are used directly, without a separate migration flow. When you have not made a choice, the switch is on by default: the app grants analytics storage and sends detailed custom app-usage and ad-performance events for product analytics and improvement.

Turn it off in Settings anytime to stop future detailed custom events and Firebase Crashlytics crash reports. While it is off, the SDK may still send limited measurement signals (app/device context, time, consent state, and automatic events). This is not a no-data-collection mode, and turning it off does not automatically delete previously processed data. Firebase handles analytics identifiers according to consent and SDK configuration. Crash reports contain the stack trace, device and OS details, and the screen name, but no user ID, email, or watchlist. We do not send your name, email, or user-entered holdings.

The analytics switch does not grant ad storage, ad user data, personalized advertising, or Apple cross-app tracking permission. Use the separate advertising privacy options and system controls for advertising choices. Google processes measurement data as described in its privacy policy. Contact admin@dappgo.com to ask about or request deletion of identifiable data; aggregated statistics that cannot identify you cannot be individually located for deletion.

iOS 研究 App：Apple 追蹤授權與廣告

Options Explorer、US Stocks 與 TW Stocks 的 iOS 版本會使用 Apple 的 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）系統視窗，詢問是否允許跨其他公司 App 與網站的廣告追蹤。授權且符合適用廣告同意選擇時，Google Mobile Ads 可能使用廣告識別碼與廣告互動資料，結合其他公司提供的資料，用於個人化廣告與廣告成效衡量。這項系統授權與 UMP 廣告選擇、詳細使用分析開關各自獨立；其中一項同意不代表其他項目也同意。

拒絕、限制或無法確認 ATT 狀態時，App 只請求非個人化廣告（不授予廣告使用者資料與個人化），市場資訊與研究功能照常使用。廣告形式包括橫幅、回到 App 時的開啟廣告、事先提示的全螢幕插頁，以及您主動選擇觀看的獎勵廣告。我們不藉其他識別方式繞過拒絕追蹤的選擇。

您可以在 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」變更授權；App 會在回到前景時重新檢查，已拒絕後不會反覆要求。Android 沒有 Apple ATT 視窗，使用 UMP 與裝置廣告控制。

iOS research apps: Apple tracking authorization and ads

The iOS versions of Options Explorer, US Stocks, and TW Stocks use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system prompt to ask about advertising tracking across other companies' apps and websites. When authorized and consistent with applicable advertising choices, Google Mobile Ads may use advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data together with data from other companies for personalized advertising and ad measurement. ATT, UMP advertising choices, and the detailed usage analytics switch are separate; permission for one does not grant the others.

When ATT is denied, restricted, or cannot be verified, the app requests only non-personalized ads (ad user data and personalization are not granted); market information and research remain usable. Ad formats are banners, an app-open ad when you return to the app, full-screen interstitials announced in advance, and rewarded ads you choose to watch. We do not use alternative identifiers to bypass a tracking refusal.

Change authorization in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app checks again on returning to the foreground and does not repeatedly ask after a refusal. Android does not show Apple's ATT prompt and uses UMP and device advertising controls.

研究 App：推播通知、購買與訂閱

推播通知（選用）：您在 App 內同意並在系統允許通知後，App 會透過 Firebase Anonymous Auth 取得一組匿名 uid（保存在裝置上），並把推播 token（iOS 為 Apple APNs token，Android 為 Google FCM token）連同平台、bundle ID、訂閱市場、語言／地區、時區、通知偏好（例如訊號變化推播開關、追蹤的代號、勿擾時段）寫入 Firestore 的 users/{uid}，用於傳送您選擇的通知。若系統已允許通知，App 啟動時會同步最新 token。uid 與 token 不包含姓名、email 或 Apple／Google 帳號。您可在 App 設定或系統設定關閉通知；如需刪除這些紀錄，請使用支援此功能的 App 內「刪除我的資料」（Options Explorer）或來信 admin@dappgo.com。

去除廣告與訂閱：購買透過 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 結帳，DappGo 不接觸信用卡或銀行資訊。RevenueCat 為我們處理購買驗證與權益，接收商店交易資訊與一組匿名 App User ID（由 RevenueCat SDK 產生，不是您的 Apple ID 或 Google 帳號）；RevenueCat 也會收到廣告曝光與收益事件，用於收益統計。

US Stocks 估值頁：讀取估值資料時，App 會在請求標頭附上 RevenueCat 匿名 App User ID，DappGo 資料服務（data.dappgo.com）據此向 RevenueCat 查詢訂閱是否有效，結果僅在伺服器記憶體暫存最多 72 小時，不用於分析或廣告。7 天免費試用不需付款資料：資料服務發出一張有簽章、含到期時間的試用憑證，保存在您裝置的鑰匙圈／安全儲存，之後隨估值請求送出；憑證不含裝置或個人識別碼。

Research apps: push notifications, purchases and subscriptions

Push notifications (optional): after you agree in the app and the system allows notifications, the app obtains an anonymous uid through Firebase Anonymous Authentication (kept on the device) and writes your push token (an Apple APNs token on iOS, a Google FCM token on Android) to users/{uid} in Firestore, together with platform, bundle ID, subscribed markets, language/region, time zone and notification preferences (for example the signal-change switch, followed tickers and quiet hours). This is used only to deliver the notifications you chose. If notifications are already allowed, the app refreshes the token at launch. The uid and token contain no name, email, or Apple/Google account. Turn notifications off in the app or in system settings; to delete these records, use the in-app "Delete my data" where available (Options Explorer) or email admin@dappgo.com.

Ad removal and subscriptions: purchases are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play; DappGo never receives card or bank details. RevenueCat processes purchase validation and entitlements for us and receives store transaction details and an anonymous App User ID generated by the RevenueCat SDK (not your Apple ID or Google account). RevenueCat also receives ad impression and revenue events for revenue reporting.

US Stocks valuation page: valuation requests carry the anonymous RevenueCat App User ID in a request header. The DappGo data service (data.dappgo.com) uses it to ask RevenueCat whether the subscription is active and keeps the answer only in server memory for up to 72 hours; it is not used for analytics or advertising. The 7-day free trial needs no payment details: the data service issues a signed trial pass with an expiry time, which is stored in your device keychain / secure storage and sent with valuation requests. The pass contains no device or personal identifier.

App 端儲存的 Firestore 集合

使用需要後端服務的 App 功能時，App 可能透過 Firebase Anonymous Auth 取得一組匿名 uid。此 uid 為 Firebase 自動產生的隨機識別碼，不包含您的姓名、email、Apple ID 或任何直接識別資訊。三款研究 App 都不會在一般瀏覽時建立此識別碼；只有開啟選用推播（或 Options Explorer 的回饋功能）時才會使用。

以下為目前使用中的集合及歷史資料說明（Firebase 為資料處理者）：

  • events/ — 部分其他股票 App 版本的匿名事件紀錄。Options Explorer 目前版本不寫入此集合；既有舊資料依原本的保留政策處理。
  • feedback/ — 使用者主動透過 App 內 Feedback 表單提交的文字意見：uid、類別、評分、訊息內容、App 版本。用於產品改善與審計紀錄。保留：3 年後刪除識別欄位。
  • users/{uid} — 推播 token（iOS：Apple APNs；Android：Google FCM）、平台、通知偏好、locale、device_tz、bundle_id、訂閱市場（markets_subscribed）、註冊時間；模擬器註冊另標記 is_simulator。token 為 Apple／Google 提供的不透明裝置識別碼，不包含姓名或帳號。保留：直到您刪除資料或 token 失效為止；關閉通知會停止發送。
  • push_log/ — 推播發送嘗試紀錄：uid、market、結果（success/failure）、timestamp。用於除錯與發送品質監測。保留：30 天以上，依除錯需要。
  • iv_history/{ticker} — 每檔 ticker 的每日 IV 快照。不與使用者綁定，無隱私影響。

Options Explorer 可在 App「設定」內點選「刪除我的資料」，立即清除上述與您 uid 相關的 Firestore 文件；US Stocks 與 TW Stocks 請來信要求刪除。如需更進階的請求（例如資料匯出、特定欄位修正），請來信 admin@dappgo.com。

Firestore collections stored on our backend

When a feature needs a backend service, the app may obtain an anonymous uid via Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This is a random identifier issued by Firebase and contains no name, email, Apple ID, or other directly-identifying information. None of the three research apps creates it during ordinary browsing; it is used only when you turn on optional push notifications (or for Options Explorer feedback).

The collections in use, and historical records (Firebase acts as data processor):

  • events/ — anonymous product-event records from some other stock-app versions. The current Options Explorer version does not write to this collection; legacy records follow their existing retention policy.
  • feedback/ — user-submitted text feedback from the in-app Feedback form: uid, category, rating, body, app version. Used for product improvement and audit. Retention: identifying fields removed after 3 years.
  • users/{uid} — push token (Apple APNs on iOS, Google FCM on Android), platform, notification preferences, locale, device_tz, bundle_id, markets_subscribed, registration time; simulator registrations are marked is_simulator. Tokens are opaque device identifiers issued by Apple or Google and contain no name or account. Retention: until you delete your data or the token becomes invalid; turning notifications off stops delivery.
  • push_log/ — record of push delivery attempts: uid, market, outcome (success/failure), timestamp. Used for debugging and delivery-quality monitoring. Retention: 30+ days as needed for debugging.
  • iv_history/{ticker} — per-ticker daily IV snapshot. Not user-scoped, no privacy impact.

In Options Explorer, tap "Delete my data" in Settings to immediately clear the Firestore documents tied to your uid; for US Stocks and TW Stocks, email us to request deletion. For more advanced requests (export, field-level correction), email admin@dappgo.com.
