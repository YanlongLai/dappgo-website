Number Merge (Number Merge - DappGo)

Number Merge does not require an account or sign-in, and the core game works offline. Game settings, personal best scores, and highest tiles use local device storage. Number Merge includes Google AdMob banner ads and an optional rewarded ad that opens only when a player chooses “Watch ad +1”. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, coarse location inferred from network information, diagnostic data, performance data, and ad-interaction data for serving, measurement, frequency control, and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on AdMob configuration, region, consent status, and device settings. DappGo does not connect this information to your name, email, or financial data.

Where user choice is required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) handles the advertising consent message. You can provide, decline, or change your choice through the device or advertising privacy controls; declining may still allow non-personalized or limited ads.

App 端儲存的 Firestore 集合

使用需要後端服務的 App 功能時，App 可能透過 Firebase Anonymous Auth 取得一組匿名 uid。此 uid 為 Firebase 自動產生的隨機識別碼，不包含您的姓名、email、Apple ID 或任何直接識別資訊。Options Explorer 目前版本不會在啟動或一般瀏覽時建立此識別碼；只有主動送出回饋或開啟選用推播時才會使用。

為了讓選用功能運作，我們在 Google Firestore 儲存以下集合（Firebase 為資料處理者）：

  • events/ — 部分其他股票 App 版本的匿名事件紀錄。Options Explorer 目前版本不寫入此集合；既有舊資料依原本的保留政策處理。

  • users/{uid} — APNs 推播裝置 token、通知偏好、locale、device_tz、bundle_id、訂閱市場（markets_subscribed）。APNs token 為 Apple 提供的每台裝置不透明識別碼，非個人識別資料。保留：直到您關閉通知或刪除資料為止。
  • push_log/ — 推播發送嘗試紀錄：uid、market、結果（success/failure）、timestamp。用於除錯與發送品質監測。保留：30 天以上，依除錯需要。
  • iv_history/{ticker} — 每檔 ticker 的每日 IV 快照。不與使用者綁定，無隱私影響。

您可隨時於 App「設定」內點選「刪除我的資料」按鈕，立即清除上述所有與您 uid 相關的 Firestore 文件；如需更進階的請求（例如資料匯出、特定欄位修正），請來信 admin@dappgo.com。

Firestore collections stored on our backend

When a feature needs a backend service, the app may obtain an anonymous uid via Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This is a random identifier issued by Firebase and contains no name, email, Apple ID, or other directly-identifying information. The current Options Explorer version does not create it at launch or during ordinary browsing; it uses it only for explicit feedback submission or optional push registration.

To support optional features, we store the following collections in Google Firestore (Firebase acts as data processor):

  • events/ — anonymous product-event records from some other stock-app versions. The current Options Explorer version does not write to this collection; legacy records follow their existing retention policy.
  • feedback/ — user-submitted text feedback from the in-app Feedback form: uid, category, rating, body, app version. Used for product improvement and audit. Retention: identifying fields removed after 3 years.
  • users/{uid} — APNs device token, notification preferences, locale, device_tz, bundle_id, markets_subscribed. The APNs token is a per-device opaque identifier issued by Apple and is not a personal identifier. Retention: until you opt out of notifications or delete your data.
  • push_log/ — record of push delivery attempts: uid, market, outcome (success/failure), timestamp. Used for debugging and delivery-quality monitoring. Retention: 30+ days as needed for debugging.
  • iv_history/{ticker} — per-ticker daily IV snapshot. Not user-scoped, no privacy impact.

You can tap the "Delete my data" button in the app's Settings at any time to immediately clear all Firestore documents tied to your uid. For more advanced requests (export, field-level correction), email admin@dappgo.com.
