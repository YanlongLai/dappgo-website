# Requirements and design decisions

State: ready-for-design. Scope: local design proposal, not production implementation.

Owner direction: improve the professional appearance of dappgo.com; explain and link US Stocks Web, TW Stocks Web, Options Web and DappGo News; include four released Android apps and the TW Stocks / Options iOS listings; use a new worktree and design interaction and technology first.

No material external decision is needed to prepare this reversible proposal. Authorization does not include merging, deployment, store metadata changes or AdSense changes. These remain subsequent work after design review. No new backend, login, paid library or live financial-data request is needed for the homepage.
