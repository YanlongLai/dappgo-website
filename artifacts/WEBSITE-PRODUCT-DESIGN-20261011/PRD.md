# DappGo product-first homepage

## Outcome

A reader can understand each product, select a market, and open the web experience or the correct store listing without hunting through contact links. The homepage communicates established products through real screens and precise descriptions.

## Current-site audit

- Existing brand: blue #245dde, Space Grotesk with system CJK fallback, rounded D icon and DappGo wordmark, light/dark themes, Chinese/English.
- Existing navigation: products, method, workflows, contact. Existing support, privacy, terms and options.html URLs must continue working.
- Current hero and product display favor Options Explorer. US/TW/Number Merge cards still send readers to contact instead of released products. News and the stock web experiences have no coherent entry points.
- Decorative process motion competes with selecting a product. New design uses user-driven tabs and native disclosures instead of autoplay.
- Keep genuine app screenshots, brand, contact destinations, semantic landmarks and keyboard focus.
- Search ranking/analytics measurements were not available in this audit; do not infer a traffic improvement. Preserve current SEO metadata during implementation and update only claims justified by the product catalog.

## Information architecture

1. Compact existing navigation, language/theme controls and mobile menu.
2. Hero: daily research across web and mobile. Primary link to product selector; secondary link to app collection. Real Options app screens as product proof.
3. Product selector: US Stocks, TW Stocks, Options Explorer and DappGo News. Each has a tailored summary, use cases, genuine screenshot and direct web link. News includes US/TW destinations.
4. App collection: four cards, with Android on every card and iOS only on TW/Options. Store names remain visible; no device detection or forced redirects.
5. Research method: compact explanation of source collection, structured research and reading. Do not imply real-time quotes or recommendations.
6. Business automation/contact: keep this secondary offering, existing destination and legal footer.

## Product copy boundaries

| Product | Professional description | Destinations |
|---|---|---|
| US Stocks | Read market breadth, sector rotation, company research and relevant news in one research workspace. | stocks.dappgo.com/us/, Google Play |
| TW Stocks | Explore Taiwan equities through daily signals, sector movement, institutional flows and company news. | stocks.dappgo.com/tw/, Google Play, App Store |
| Options Explorer | Compare put-side/call-side research, option-chain context and valuation alongside the underlying company. | options.dappgo.com/us/, Google Play, App Store |
| DappGo News | Browse published RSS news by market and stock, newest first; open original reporting for the full story. | news.dappgo.com, /us/, /tw/ |
| Number Merge | A short-session number puzzle built around placing tiles, combining matches and planning the next move. | Google Play |

Use original copy, not copied store descriptions. No promised returns, unsupported scale or reliability statistics. Product screenshots are dated examples, never live quotes. News translation availability is source-dependent; do not promise every article exists in both languages.

## Acceptance

- All four Web entries and six requested store links are directly usable.
- All five products have distinct, understandable explanations.
- Keyboard and touch can operate navigation, product selection, language, theme and FAQ.
- All primary actions are reachable on 360px mobile screens without horizontal page scrolling.
- Full Chinese/English labels and coherent light/dark themes.
- No production change, ad request, new tracking, form submission, deployment or store operation in this phase.
- Deliver a reviewable local prototype, UI specification, implementation plan and evidence.
