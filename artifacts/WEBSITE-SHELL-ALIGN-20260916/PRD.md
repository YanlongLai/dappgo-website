# Product requirements

## Problem

The homepage and Privacy page had moved to the new DappGo shell, while
Options, Terms, Support, and 404 still exposed older navigation and footer
patterns. The visual split made the public site feel unfinished and made
mobile navigation inconsistent.

## Acceptance criteria

- All four remaining public pages render the current DappGo logo and shell.
- Options keeps its live production report, bilingual rendering, history link,
  disclaimer, and report-specific explanatory content.
- Terms keeps its bilingual legal content and disclaimer.
- Support keeps its FAQ, Google feedback/contact forms, and email path.
- 404 keeps a clear recovery path to the homepage.
- Desktop and mobile layouts have no horizontal overflow.
- Theme, locale, and mobile navigation controls are keyboard-accessible.
- Existing canonical routes and favicon remain valid.
