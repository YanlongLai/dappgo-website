# Product requirement

News AI viewpoint must present report commentary for the selected ticker and requested language. It must not display source-headline excerpts or reusable validation checklists **as the company view**. When the optional source plan was accepted, a separately labelled publisher context can accompany a real company view. A missing company view hides the whole card. All-market analysis remains separately scoped. Links to publishers remain in story cards.

The current production US payload contains model-selected news source excerpts plus per-ticker quantitative commentary. The former is wrongly preferred by the web client. TW already publishes substantive mapped per-ticker commentary. This work first fixes that contract mismatch and separately tracks the US engine's repetitive analysis quality.
