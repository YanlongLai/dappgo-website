Number RPG by DappGo

Delete your account

Instructions effective: 2026-10-27

You can request deletion in the app or by email. The in-app flow is the fastest way to delete the Number RPG account and its player-owned data.

Delete in the app

Open Number RPG and go to Settings → Delete account. Read the confirmation, confirm the deletion, and keep the app online until it reports success. If an Apple identity is linked, the app/API may require Apple re-authorisation before deletion can finish.

Request deletion by email

Email admin@dappgo.com and ask to delete your Number RPG account. Include enough information for DappGo to identify the account, such as the in-game display name and account ID if available. Do not send a password, provider access token, or Apple private key. We may ask for verification before acting on an email request.

What the API deletes

A successful deletion removes the player account and the player-owned records linked by the API: provider identities, device and session records, cloud saves, scores, invites, friendships, gifts, rewards, events, activity, purchase records, fraud flags, and push tokens. Local game progress is reset by the app. Store purchases are handled by Apple or Google and remain restorable where the store and entitlement allow it.

How long it takes

The current DELETE /me implementation creates a deletion request, performs any required Apple revocation, deletes the player data, marks the request completed, and returns success in the same server operation. There is no code-defined waiting period for a successful in-app request. A network failure or required Apple re-authorisation can prevent completion and requires retrying.

The completed deletion-request record keeps its request and completion timestamps while its player reference is cleared. Related admin audit entries are anonymised. The code does not define a separate deadline for a manually verified email request; Owner review is required for that procedure.

Need help?

Contact admin@dappgo.com if the in-app flow fails, you cannot access the account, or you need to ask about an email request.
