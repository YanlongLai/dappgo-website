# Homepage store artwork

Downloaded 2026-10-11; official SVG artwork is stored unmodified.

- App Store: https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg
- Apple usage guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
- Google Play: English digital SVG from the badge download archive linked by https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/
  Archive member: Google Play Badge guidelines/Get it on Google Play Badges/Digital/svg/GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_English.svg

Black badges are used together, App Store first. Both are scaled proportionally to 44 CSS pixels high with surrounding clear space. Unavailable iOS apps use an unbranded noninteractive iOS / Coming soon placeholder. The earlier Vecteezy JPEG crops remain for the historical prototype only; the production homepage no longer uses them. Do not add wrapper borders or padding to available badges, recolor their artwork, or place status text inside them.
